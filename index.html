Privacy Policy — Patente B Italia

Effective date: 5 November 2026
Developer: Syed Wajahat Attari
Contact: syedwajahatattari@gmail.com

Patente B Italia ("the App") helps you prepare for the Italian driving licence (Patente B) exam with quizzes, video lessons and study articles. This policy explains what data the App and its third-party services handle.

1. No account, no login

The App does not require an account, a login, or any personal information from you to use it. There is no profile, no username, and no password.

2. Data stored on your device only

Your learning progress — quiz results, error bank, watched video lessons, articles marked as read, app language and theme — is stored locally on your device (SharedPreferences / local storage). It never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. We do not receive, store or see any of it.

3. Advertising in the free version (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device advertising ID,
  • device information (model, operating system, language),
  • approximate location (derived from your IP address) to serve relevant ads,
  • interaction data (whether an ad was shown or tapped).

You can opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID, or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"). PRO users see no ads at all — upgrading to PRO removes advertising from the App.

AdMob's data practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Purchases — PRO (Google Play and RevenueCat)

PRO is sold as an in-app purchase / subscription through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google; we never see or store your card number or bank details.

To verify your PRO status the App uses RevenueCat. When you buy or restore PRO, RevenueCat receives:

  • a randomly generated, anonymous app user ID,
  • your purchase history for this App (product, purchase date, expiry date),
  • your device type and App version.

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, or payment details. Its privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

5. Learning content served from the internet

  • Sign images and video lessons are loaded from public content hosted on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). When the App loads them, GitHub receives a normal web request (including your IP address), exactly as when you open any website. No personal data is attached to these requests.
  • Fonts: the App downloads the Outfit font from Google Fonts at runtime. Google receives a normal web request (including your IP address) for the font files.
  • Articles: the study articles are bundled inside the App and work offline. The App does not send your questions or answers to any AI service.

6. What we do NOT collect

  • No location tracking (the App never asks for your location).
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No analytics or usage tracking SDKs.
  • No data is sold to anyone, ever.

7. Children

The App is a driving-licence study aid for learners preparing for the Patente B exam (adults). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Your choices

  • Delete local data: clear the App's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the App.
  • Ads: opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (see section 3).
  • Purchases: manage or cancel a PRO subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Questions or requests about this policy: email syedwajahatattari@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
